# Candidate Profile — Carlos Fernando Perez Botero

## Who
Global creative leader with 20+ years helping the world's most influential
brands stay culturally relevant, creatively ambitious, and unmistakably
themselves. Born in Colombia, career built in the US; fully bilingual
(English/Spanish). Currently Senior Creative Director at Airbnb.

## Track record (verified)
- **Airbnb — Senior Creative Director** (2023–present): leads creative for
  global expansion across LATAM, US Hispanic, Canada, EMEA, APAC. Built the
  multidisciplinary in-house team (design, art direction, writing, motion, UX).
  Helped launch Airbnb Services and the reimagined Airbnb Experiences. Led
  development of Airbnb's AI operating model for creative.
- **Ogilvy — Global Executive Creative Director** (2022–2023): led the global
  Samsung Mobile creative business from New York; 50+ person team; launches
  across the Galaxy ecosystem; multimillion-dollar productions across US, UK,
  Germany, Japan, Brazil, Mexico, Australia; daily partnership with Samsung
  HQ in Seoul.
- **Publicis — Global Group Creative Director** (2020–2022): won the global
  Infiniti pitch, then built and led the worldwide creative team; connected
  global brand vision to launches, campaigns, and retail across markets.
- **TBWA\Media Arts Lab — Group Creative Director** (2018–2019): built MAL's
  LATAM creative hub for Apple from zero, embedded in Apple's Miami office;
  iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch launches and @apple content across Mexico,
  Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Chile; worked with Marcom leadership in
  Cupertino.
- **AKQA — Creative Director** (2014–2016): Verizon's flagship Destination
  Stores (concept through deployment: UX, motion, software, retail ops);
  pitched and won Clinique for the network; led Nike Running and Yoga.

## Signature strengths
1. **Global ideas that feel locally true** — creates across languages,
   cultures, and markets; LATAM and multicultural briefs are a distinctive edge.
2. **Executive-level partnership** — has set creative vision alongside senior
   leadership at Apple, Samsung, Airbnb, and Nissan at moments when the
   direction of the brand was at stake.
3. **Building what doesn't exist yet** — teams, operating models, standards,
   and creative cultures from zero (MAL LATAM, Airbnb expansion team,
   Samsung's global operating model).
4. **AI-era creative leadership** — designed and led an AI operating model
   for a world-class in-house creative org; believes the more powerful
   technology becomes, the more deeply human its voice must be.

## What "great fit" looks like (score high)
- Senior creative/brand leadership: Creative Director, Group CD, Executive CD,
  Head of Creative, Head of Brand, VP Brand/Creative
- Brand-led scope: brand platforms, campaigns, brand studio, launches,
  storytelling — owning creative output and the team behind it
- AI-native companies, or design-led tech with real craft culture
- Global / multi-market remits; building or transforming a creative function;
  consumer tech, platforms, and culture-shaping brands
- Roles where the brand is still being invented or reinvented for the AI era

## What "weak fit" looks like (score low)
- Roles at **Airbnb** (current employer — not looking internally)
- Individual-contributor or hands-on-only roles below his seniority
- Pure product-design / UX leadership (his craft is brand, campaigns, and
  integrated experiences — not product UI orgs)
- Pure PR/communications, performance marketing, or sales-adjacent roles
- "Creative" roles unrelated to brand craft (e.g., retail floor positions)
